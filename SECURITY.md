# Security

Found a way to read someone's games, get around the passcode lock, or read or change a game on the TV relay without its code? Please tell us privately at [wyzie.io/contact](https://wyzie.io/contact) instead of opening a public issue, and give us a chance to fix it before you share it.

Helpful to include: what you found, the steps to see it happen, and which part it's in (the site at pitmaster.cc, or the TV relay at api.pitmaster.cc).

The site's own copy of this is at [pitmaster.cc/.well-known/security.txt](https://pitmaster.cc/.well-known/security.txt). How PitMaster keeps data encrypted is described on its [Privacy](https://pitmaster.cc/privacy) page.
